### Phase 1 — literature review, datasets, solution concept (10 pts)

| # | Criterion | Pts | Full credit means |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **1.1** | **State of the art** | 3 | At least 15 works, each with what it does, on what data, with what result. A table comparing published numbers, with the dataset and split named for every number. |
| **1.2** | **Datasets and their status** | 3 | Every dataset: size, label definition, class balance, licence, how obtained. Labelled `READY` / `TO COLLECT` / `RESTRICTED`. If `TO COLLECT`: how many items, by when, annotated by whom. |
| **1.3** | **Solution concept and experimental plan** | 2 | Pipeline in one figure, metrics chosen before results exist, 2–3 concrete success deliverables. Week-by-week plan with named owners. |
| **1.4** | **Text quality and references** | 1 | Scientific language, no unexplained abbreviations. Cite the published version of every work that has one; preprint-only citations under one third of the list. |
| **1.5** | **Data statement (new)** | 1 | Where the data comes from, licence, whether it contains personal data, and what you do about it. Six lines are enough. |
